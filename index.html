Published Research

Analytical Views on Stress and Its Impact on Mental Health

International Journal of Research Publication and Reviews · Vol. 6, Issue 1 · January 2025

Led the study end to end: designed a 7 question Google Forms survey, distributed it through WhatsApp and Facebook, collected 50 responses, analyzed the data, built the charts and wrote the manuscript.

  • 52% of respondents were most stressed at work, with afternoons the peak time (44%).
  • 92% said stress affects their daily life at least sometimes.
  • 60% use medication for stress relief at least occasionally, a clear role for pharmacists.
